# Husainprompts — Axe launch-film playbook

Read this file before changing the Axe launch video. It preserves the production
strategy extracted from Husain's Codex prompts and the visual decisions locked
during the Axe review.

## The film's single claim

**Replying is how small creators grow on X.**

More specifically: find conversations that already have attention, add something
useful, and repeat consistently. Posting alone is not a second thesis and should
not consume screen time.

## Current authoritative cut — 30 seconds / 900 frames

This section overrides the older screenplay notes below whenever they conflict.
The film is one continuous X session with one persistent viewport and one camera.
X navigation, rails, timeline structure and native separators remain spatially
coherent until the final Axe card. A new screen must be caused by an X action or
navigation move; it must never feel like a collection of presentation slides.

1. Open on one X timeline containing familiar Sahil Bloom and Justin Welsh posts.
   Push into the native views count on Justin's post and hold on `112K`.
2. Pull back to the same timeline. A native in-X message toast from `Co-founder`
   reads `i also wanna get 112K views`, is held long enough to read, then swipes
   away.
3. Travel through that same timeline and push into Sahil's native `774K` views.
4. Return to Justin's post about fast-growing accounts engaging regularly. Follow
   the native reply icon into the native composer.
5. Type exactly: `Small creators: replying is the way to grow on X.` Click the
   native Reply button.
6. Follow the posted reply into the thread. Its real X engagement row climbs from
   a few views through `1.2K`, `10.4K`, and `48K`; every jump brings the camera
   closer. Metrics never detach from the post into headline graphics.
7. Navigate to native X Notifications. Show Sahil liking the reply, new followers,
   and profile visits as real notification rows. Reframe each row cleanly.
8. Return to the native feed and scroll through repeated replies. Several native
   X reply fragments accumulate in a scrambled stack, each marked `You replied ✓`.
9. Navigate to native X analytics and hold on millions of impressions for the
   last 30 days.
10. Navigate to the native profile. Followers rise visibly from `286` through
    `742`, `1.8K`, `3.2K`, and `5.1K`.
11. Navigate back to Notifications. Push into the native row: `Your post got 112K
    views`. This is the callback and final growth payoff.
12. Blur/dim the still-persistent X world for the only thesis overlay:
    `REPLY. BE USEFUL. REPEAT.` Then cut to the Axe end card.

No external visual appears before the end card. The only permitted non-X visual
elements are the thesis text and final Axe brand lockup. Sound is synchronized to
camera landings, the reply click, metric jumps, notification arrivals, repeated
reply stacking, follower milestones, the 112K callback, thesis, and brand cut.

## Locked visual language

- Rebuild X as DOM. Never screen-record it.
- X dark mode is the world of the film, not a decorative frame around slides.
- Use the native tokens: `#000000`, `#16181c`, `#e7e9ea`, `#71767b`,
  `#2f3336`, X blue `#1d9bf0`, repost green `#00ba7c`, like pink `#f91880`.
- Use the native system font stack and native post anatomy: rails, separators,
  round avatars, bylines, action rows, thread stems, composer, notifications.
- Cinematic energy comes from camera movement, scale, cuts, legibility and sound.
  Never introduce neon paths, glass cards, invented dashboards, glows or graphs.
- Familiar names, avatars, post text and public metrics must be verified. If a
  source cannot be checked, remove or anonymize it instead of inventing a quote.
- Real assets live in `public/x-reference/`; the temporary API key is never saved.

## Husain method

1. One feature owns the film. Secondary capabilities get one brief UI beat.
2. Build one virtual stage larger than the frame and move one camera over it.
3. Name camera placements by subject: `wideFeed`, `heroPost`, `replyComposer`,
   `replyMetrics`, `notificationPayoff`, `opportunityWall`, `axeReveal`.
4. Frame the readable text run, not the panel containing it.
5. At 16:9, retain enough left rail, center timeline and right rail to unmistakably
   read as X; close-ups may crop chrome only when the viewer already knows the space.
6. Transform the stage with `scale(...) translate(...)`. A zoom must preserve its
   subject instead of drifting into empty stage.
7. Legibility sets the pace. The operative word or metric gets X-native color.
8. When one item is not enough, multiply instead of cycling. Open on one readable
   real object, then pull back exponentially into a field of hundreds. Use real
   content nearest the lens and skeleton content in the distance.
9. Centered copy gets its own visual ground: blur/dim the X stage behind it. Text
   shadows alone are insufficient.
10. Work on a 120 BPM grid at 30 fps: 15 frames per beat, 60 frames per bar.
    Important cuts happen on beat boundaries. Duck 10–12 frames before a major hit.
11. Repeated visual events use rising musical stabs, not identical clicks.
12. Render silent visuals and master the score separately when iterating heavily.
    Final target: approximately `-14 LUFS`, true peak no higher than `-1 dB`.
13. Review keyframes before the full render, then verify dimensions, fps, duration,
    black frames and audible stereo output.

## Premium-motion rules for Axe

- A camera move must reveal meaning. Do not zoom merely because a scene changed.
- Slow push: attention is gathering. Snap push: the viewer has discovered the
  operative object. Pullback: scale or repetition is being revealed.
- The reply being typed is a held, readable shot. It is the emotional decision.
- The send click is a cut point: short pre-hit duck, tactile click, then impact.
- As likes, views and followers rise, progressively crop tighter on the native
  metrics. The payoff must occupy the frame; do not leave it at wide-feed scale.
- The infinity beat represents thousands of conversations already happening on X.
  It is a sea of native post/thread surfaces, never abstract cards.
- The Axe UI appears only after the problem and mechanism are felt. It is the
  instrument that finds the opportunities, not the hero before the thesis lands.

## Revised premium screenplay — 26 seconds / 780 frames

### 0:00–0:02 — Attention already exists (frames 0–60)

**Picture:** Begin inside a tight crop of a real X engagement row. The views number
is readable. Camera pulls back just enough to reveal Justin Welsh's real post in a
full X timeline, with the left and right rails entering parallax.

**Copy:** `THE ATTENTION IS ALREADY HERE.` centered, not at the bottom. X is dimmed
and softly blurred for the 24–30 frames in which the line is fully readable.

**Sound:** Sparse pulse, one breath of space, impact on frame 60.

### 0:02–0:05 — Find the opening (frames 60–150)

**Picture:** Feed accelerates vertically, then brakes hard on the Justin post.
Camera performs a snap push into `3. Deep network: They engage regularly`, then
travels down to the native reply icon. The icon receives only the real X-blue hover
state.

**Copy:** `DON'T SCROLL PAST IT.` centered during the braking moment.

**Sound:** Hats track the scroll. Ten-frame duck before the feed lock. Tight impact
when the reply icon becomes the focal point.

### 0:05–0:10 — Add something useful (frames 150–300)

**Picture:** The native reply composer expands from the exact icon position rather
than appearing as a disconnected modal. Hold the camera still enough to read the
reply being typed. On the final phrase, make a gentle 6–8% push into the words
`where people are already paying attention.` The Reply button fires on frame 278.

**Copy:** No external title. The typed reply carries the idea.

**Sound:** Minimal low pulse beneath typing. Keystrokes form a rising phrase. Duck
before frame 278, tactile click, hard impact, two-frame white exposure lift.

### 0:10–0:14 — The reply enters the room (frames 300–420)

**Picture:** Follow the posted reply downward into the native thread. Begin wide
enough to retain the parent post, then push progressively tighter as the native
metrics change: `186 views` → `3.2K`, `0 likes` → `8` → `24`. Each change moves the
camera closer; the final pink heart and count occupy the dominant visual quadrant.

**Copy:** `ONE USEFUL REPLY.` centered over a dimmed/blurred hold only after the
viewer has seen the reply land.

**Sound:** Each metric jump is a rising pitched stab. The last jump gets the kick.

### 0:14–0:18 — Discovery (frames 420–540)

**Picture:** A whip-pan lands on native X Notifications. Likes arrive as native
rows—pink heart, avatars, separators—not floating toasts. Camera successively
reframes the liked reply, profile views, and new followers. Finish on the profile
avatar plus follower count, close enough that the number is unavoidable.

**Copy:** `PUTS YOU IN THE ROOM.` centered. Background dims and blurs while the
notifications remain faintly moving behind it.

**Sound:** Four rising notification stabs across the bar. Strongest impact on the
follower change, not the first like.

### 0:18–0:22 — Infinity / consistency (frames 540–660)

**Picture:** Start on one readable post-and-reply thread. Pull back exponentially:
one becomes nine, nine becomes dozens, dozens become a seemingly endless plane of
native X conversations. The nearest real cells use verified avatars/posts; distant
cells become accurate X skeletons. Camera travels backward through depth while new
reply marks light in X blue across the field in a wave.

**Copy:** `REPLY. BE USEFUL. REPEAT.` centered on a black-to-transparent ground.

**Meaning:** Growth is not one viral reply. It is consistently entering many rooms
where attention already exists.

**Sound:** The score reaches maximum density. Reply lights trigger ascending stabs.
Duck the final 12 frames to create space for the product reveal.

### 0:22–0:24 — Axe finds the rooms (frames 660–720)

**Picture:** The infinite field collapses back into a full X layout. The real Axe
sidebar slides into its native location and identifies the same familiar creators
and high-attention posts. One concise action: `Reply while it's moving.` Camera
pushes into that CTA, then pulls back to the complete X + Axe composition.

**Copy:** None. Product UI is the line.

**Sound:** Clean product-reveal impact, then rhythm strips down.

### 0:24–0:26 — Brand resolve (frames 720–780)

**Picture:** Hard cut to black. Axe lockup, then the final thesis. No decorative
particles, orbit, glow or abstract iconography.

**Copy:** `AXE` / `Reply your way to growth.` / `axe.oddpages.site`

**Sound:** Low resolved note, one restrained high accent, controlled tail.

## Approved 112K revision

- Preserve the screenplay above; do not replace its narrative with a new concept.
- The opening post now holds on `112K VIEWS`, followed by `WANT 112K VIEWS?`.
- The typed reply is: `Small creators: replying is the way to grow on X.`

  `YOUR POSTS CAN HIT 112K TOO.` and then continue the existing screenplay.
- Show growth over time from `286` followers through `742`, `1.8K`, and `5.1K`.
- In the repetition montage, the explicit completed-action motif is
  `YOU REPLIED ✓`, never the vague label `useful reply`.
- Do not hesitate to zoom, pull out, travel, or scroll. One metric, notification,

  readable X conversations and three beat-synced `YOU REPLIED ✓` confirmations.
- The Axe opportunity UI receives a large hero treatment, not a tiny dashboard.

## Review contract

Before approving another master, inspect at minimum frames 30, 90, 145, 220, 278,
330, 390, 455, 515, 570, 620, 690, 735 and 775. Specifically reject:

- constant camera scale across a payoff;
- copy sitting unnoticed at the bottom edge;
